# Landing page (wwwroot/landing.html, served at /)

Scope: the public landing page. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: all AEC professionals working with Revit models. Action: open the sample model in /app and ask a question as a guest (no sign-up). Proof: real answers from the sample models only (no accuracy figures, no customers). Constraints: Claude's palette, EN/FR toggle, a question-and-answer demo, never show the internal query; no black nav bar (owner removed it).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the printed hoarding around a live building site, and the product is what you watch through the viewing window cut into it. It refuses the category default of a headline-and-screenshot split hero over a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Clay (#c15f3c) hoarding panels own the page as full-width fields with vertical panel seams; ink (#141413) billboard type in Barlow Semi Condensed; ivory sign boards bolted onto the panels; site signage grammar: deep-blue mandatory signs for what you can ask, clay prohibition signs (ring and slash) for what the data cannot answer; a dense small-print project board at the foot.

STORY: The visitor sees a question asked at billboard scale and the model answering through the window, understands that answers are exact and shown in 3D, sees what can and cannot be asked as site signs, and walks through the site entrance into the sample model.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Clay field edge to edge. Nav set into the top of the hoarding (logo, links, EN/FR, ivory "Open the app"). Left: the headline at billboard scale, lede, and the entrance action (ink button) with the secondary link. Right: a large round viewing window cut into the panel (inset depth), showing the isometric frame; each demo question irises in through the window, the question types on a sign strip at the window's foot, understood tags stamp in, elements light in clay, and the answer is pinned to them with a leader line. Raises: one shouting hierarchy and a small-print project board (exploitation one-sheet); iris transitions through the window (rubber-hose cel); answers pinned with leader lines (tensegrity); one clock for every beat (drum machine).

FORM: The Site Hoarding, candidate 4 of 7 on the ordered list (scale model, steel handbook, Swiss poster, site hoarding, calc pad, schedule, drawing sheet); seed key e77df6e2.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
